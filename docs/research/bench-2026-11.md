# M5 bench, 2026-11 (Vulkan vs CUDA, audio defaults, reference phrases)

Numbers only. No phrase text, audio or dictionary words are in this file.

## Setup

- Machine: RTX 4070 SUPER 12 GB (driver 617.42, CUDA toolkit 13.5), Ryzen 7 9800X3D, Vulkan device 1 is the AMD iGPU (never picked: `--gpu auto` chose the NVIDIA one in every run).
- Binary: `OxiYapper bench <dir> --model <f> --prompt-file <dict> --lang ru --gpu auto --vram`, release builds at commit `80ddb49` plus the hidden bench flags below. whisper-rs 0.16, greedy sampling, 4 decode threads on GPU.
- Not a quiet box: the owner's resident whisper-server and normal desktop use shared the GPU. GPU utilisation just before each run was 5-84 % (listed per row below as "util before"); VRAM base was about 5.7 GB while the owner's model sat in VRAM and about 1.0 GB after it was evicted (see OOM). Treat differences under about 10 % as noise.
- Models, SHA256 checked against `oxi_core::models` (the bench refuses a registry name with a wrong hash): podlodka f16 and large-v3 were the owner's existing files (copied, not re-downloaded; `ggml-large-v3.bin` in the owner's folder is podlodka, the `.original-backup` is large-v3), turbo downloaded from the registry URL (1.6 GB).
- Latency set: 31 owner clips that gave audio (one more, 0.7 s with peak 54/32768, is correctly `NothingHeard`): 1.0 s min, 9.2 s p50, 26.9 s p95, 50.5 s max, 370 s total. It contains the 8 reference phrases. p50/p95 are transcribe-time percentiles over those 31 clips, per run; table cells are the median of 3 runs with min-max in brackets. Prompt: the owner's 58-word curated dictionary.
- Hidden bench flags added in `src/bench.rs` (not in the usage text): `--no-trim`, `--no-normalize`, `--denoise`, `--cut-end-ms <n>`, `--vram`.
- Flash attention is not exposed by `oxi-whisper` (`WhisperContextParameters::flash_attn` defaults to false in whisper-rs 0.16, the log shows `flash attn = 0`). The FA rows come from a scratch copy of the tree outside the repo with `params.flash_attn(true)` in `oxi-whisper::open`; the repo is unchanged there.

### Verdict: DROP CUDA

CUDA is faster by 12-22 % at p50, which is 24 ms (turbo) to 147 ms (large-v3) with flash attention on, against p50 values of 0.2-0.7 s on a 9 s median clip. Turning flash attention on for Vulkan, one line in `oxi-whisper::open`, gives Vulkan -20 to -41 % and puts it level with the CUDA build that has it off. SPEC asks for a "clear win" to keep CUDA; 93 ms at p50 on the default-candidate model is not one, and the cost is a second toolchain, a CUDA runtime to ship, and a CI job. Counter-point: the p95 gap on long clips is 0.14-0.82 s and CUDA frees VRAM more completely on unload (0.1-0.2 GB left against 0.4-1.0 GB).

## Forced GPU OOM

CUDA only. Four podlodka processes started 3 s apart (about 14.4 GB of weights and buffers, plus the owner's 5.7 GB resident, on a 12.3 GB card): no process aborted, none printed an error or fell back to CPU. All four loaded on `CUDA0` and then crawled: none finished 31 clips in 200 s (single process: 20-25 s), so the 200 s limit killed them (exit 124). `nvidia-smi` showed 11.5 GB used, so the over-subscription went to system memory (the NVIDIA driver's sysmem fallback, a driver setting that is on by default here). With two processes (about 13 GB needed) both finished, p50 1.7 s instead of 0.65 s.
Consequences: an out-of-memory failure on this machine is a slowdown, not a crash; whisper.cpp's own abort path was not reached. Side effect: after the probe the owner's resident whisper-server dropped from about 5.7 GB to about 1 GB of VRAM while its process stayed alive (the driver evicted it; it comes back on its next use). The probe was not repeated, and the Vulkan OOM path was not probed for that reason. Whether a GPU error on another machine or driver setting aborts the process is unknown from this run; `Engine::load` already retries on CPU for errors that are returned.

Winners:

- normalise: keep on. Off changes 4 of 31 (podlodka) and 3 of 31 (turbo) clips, scores 26 vs 25 and 17 vs 16: a tie within noise, no gain from turning it off.
- trim: keep on. Podlodka ties (25 vs 25) but individual phrases move in both directions (p2 2 to 6, p4 3 to 1, p7 3 to 1); turbo is better with trim on (16 vs 19). Latency effect is within run spread (whisper pads to 30 s windows): podlodka p50 699 vs 658 ms, turbo 327 vs 274 ms, single runs.
- denoise: keep off. Podlodka 24 vs 25 is a tie; turbo is worse (21 vs 16), and 12-13 of 31 clips change text.
- tail: the file bench cannot change the real release tail, so it cuts the end of recorded clips instead (the owner's clips already carry the previous tool's tail). 100 ms off costs nothing measurable (26 vs 25, 3 clips change); 300 ms off costs +8 edits and 13 clips change; 600 ms off costs +10 and 24 clips change. Keep `tail_ms = 100`; going below it is safe on these clips, going above is untested here (no extra audio exists to append).
- Latency rows for the A/B runs are single runs and are not used.

## Audio gate check (`oxi_core::audio` trim, `HOLD_FRAMES = 6`)

- `NothingHeard` on the 8 reference phrases: 0 of 8 (all 8 reach whisper with trim on). Over the 31 clips: 1 `NothingHeard` (the 0.7 s near-silent clip, peak 54/32768), correct.
- Start/end cut: trim on vs off changes the text of 3 of 8 phrases (podlodka) and 3 of 8 (turbo); first word differs in 0, last word in 1 (podlodka) and 0 (turbo). First/last word against the reference: trim on 2 and 2 wrong, trim off 2 and 1 wrong (podlodka); 2 and 0 for both (turbo). The one last-word difference is the only visible end effect; edit totals are equal (25 vs 25) so the gate does not cut speech on these 8.

## 8 reference phrases

- Against the reference texts (strict equality after folding): 0/8 equal for podlodka, large-v3 and 1/8 for turbo in every configuration, so "equal to the reference" never holds; word edits 25 (podlodka, 18 %), 24 (large-v3, 17 %), 16 (turbo, 11.5 %) of 139. The owner's bench reported WER 19.9 % (podlodka), 15.6 % (large-v3, new dictionary), 9.9 % (turbo, new dictionary) on the same set: the same order and about the same size.
- Against the owner's bench output for the same audio (the saved whisper-server result for podlodka with its request fields), strict equality: with trim and normalise off (CUDA, no FA), 8/8 equal (0 word edits); with the default pipeline 4/8 (11 edits); trim off only: 7/8 (1 edit); normalise off only: 4/8; denoise on: 5/8 (4 edits). So the engine reproduces the owner's bench exactly, and the 4 differing phrases under defaults come from trim and normalise, with a total reference-score difference of 1 edit (25 vs 26) in favour of the defaults. Decoder thresholds (entropy 2.8, logprob -1.25, the owner's request fields) were tried with the default pipeline and change nothing (same counts, same edits, all three models).
- large-v3 against the owner's bench output with the same dictionary: 5/8 equal (7 edits) with the default pipeline; turbo against the owner's turbo output: 5/8 on CUDA, 4/8 on Vulkan (4 and 5 edits).
- Vulkan and CUDA give the same 8-phrase counts for podlodka and large-v3 (the same edits to the reference); turbo has 17 edits on Vulkan against 16 on CUDA.
- ROADMAP M5 "all 8 phrases give the same text as the owner's bench" holds only with trim and normalise off. With the defaults it is 4/8 for podlodka, with equal quality against the reference.

## Changes the numbers call for

1. `oxi-whisper::open`: `params.flash_attn(true)` (not exposed today). Vulkan p50 -20 to -41 %, 8-phrase score unchanged within +-3 edits. whisper-rs says flash attention disables DTW; the app does not use DTW.
2. CI: remove the Windows CUDA job (it is non-blocking until M5 decides); keep Vulkan blocking. Revisit only if a later model makes the 14-22 % matter.
3. Config defaults in `oxi-core::config`: no change (normalise on, trim on, denoise off, tail 100 ms are the winners or ties).
4. Decide whether ROADMAP's "same text as the owner's bench" should be checked with trim/normalise off (8/8) or with defaults (4/8, equal quality); the pipeline changes text on 4 of 8 phrases.
5. Do not run a second GPU-filling process on this box while the owner's server is resident; see the OOM section.
